Lesson 35: Giving Basic Opinions

Like / don't like + -ing · Grammar Bridge: Present Continuous · 45 min self-study

Talk about what you like and don't like at work
Hablar de lo que te gusta y no te gusta en el trabajo
Use like, love, enjoy, hate + -ing correctly
Usar like, love, enjoy, hate + -ing correctamente
Tell “I'm working” from “I like working”
Diferenciar “I'm working” de “I like working”
