Lesson 39: Cross-Cultural Greetings

Polite expressions review · Grammar Bridge: Be + from · 45 minutes

Say where people and companies are from
Decir de dónde son personas y empresas
Use countries and nationalities with capital letters
Usar países y nacionalidades con mayúscula
Name greeting gestures, names and titles
Nombrar gestos de saludo, nombres y títulos
Tell formal from informal greetings
Distinguir saludos formales e informales
Use Could I, Could you, May I and Would you like politely
Usar expresiones de cortesía
Understand a short talk about greeting customs
Entender un audio sobre costumbres de saludo
