Lesson 45: Workplace Safety

Must / mustn't · Grammar Bridge: should / shouldn't · approx. 45 min

Understand safety rules and signs at work
Entender las normas y los letreros de seguridad
Use must and mustn't to explain rules
Usar must y mustn't para explicar normas
Tell the difference between a rule and advice
Diferenciar una norma (must) de un consejo (should)
