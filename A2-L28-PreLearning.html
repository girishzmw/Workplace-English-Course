Lesson 28: Company Policies

Must, mustn't and be allowed to · Grammar bridge: have to / need to

Explain the rules at your company
Explicar las normas de tu empresa
Say what is required, prohibited, allowed or optional
Decir qué es obligatorio, prohibido, permitido u opcional
Ask about rules politely with "Am I allowed to...?"
Preguntar por las normas con cortesía
